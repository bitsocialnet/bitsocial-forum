# Product

## What it is

Bitsocial Forum is the official discussion space for the Bitsocial ecosystem: the protocol, the BSO token and Bitsocial Chain, the clients, the challenges, the bots and the infrastructure. It is a Bitsocial client, so the forum itself runs on the protocol it discusses.

## Who it is for

- **Contributors and maintainers** of Bitsocial projects who need a durable place for design discussions, proposals and bug triage instead of Telegram threads and tweets.
- **Users of Seedit and 5chan** looking for support, release notes and a way to give feedback.
- **BSO holders and node operators** following the token, chain and infrastructure work.

## Principles

- **No global admins.** Sections are directory codes that communities compete for. Whoever runs a section's community moderates that section only, and can be replaced.
- **Works if we disappear.** If every community run by the Bitsocial team goes offline, anyone can create communities for the same sections and the client keeps working.
- **A real forum.** Long-lived topics, flat chronological threads, categories and tags, the way Discourse works, rather than a feed or an imageboard.
- **One identity across the ecosystem.** Accounts are Bitsocial accounts. Profiles will be author communities, hosted by Forge RPC by default and movable to the user's own node.

## Out of scope for now

Private messages, notifications by email, and any server-side component in this repository.
